Delete your account and its data

This page is an English translation, provided for convenience. In case of any difference, the French version prevails (en cas de différence, la version française fait foi): Supprimer son compte.

You can delete your Deep Mine Idle MMO account whenever you want, for free. Deletion is immediate and permanent: nothing can be recovered afterwards.

In the game

  1. Open the game and click on your username to open My account.
  2. At the bottom of the window, click Delete my account.
  3. Confirm: type your username and password again. For a guest account, type the word SUPPRIMER.

If you no longer have access to the game

Write to contact@deepmineidle.fr giving your username or your account ID (it looks like 7K3Q-9XHP).

What is deleted

  • Your account: username, encrypted password, recovery code, ID, logins.
  • All your progress: inventory, furnace, crusher, gear, skills, spells, sets, glyphs, energy, shrines, prison, preferences and the history of your sessions.
  • Your social data: friendships and requests (on both sides), block list, presence, your place in your guild and in your party.
  • Your global chat messages and their bubbles in the Pithead, as well as the private messages you received.
  • Your records and scores in the leaderboards. In other players' records where you were a teammate, your username becomes “Joueur supprimé” (deleted player).
  • Your moderation record (warnings, sanctions).

If you are in a session with other players, you leave it and they carry on. Your name disappears from that session as soon as it ends.

What is kept

  • Reports about you, with the reported text, for player safety. In the reports you sent, your name is removed.
  • One log line: “account <ID> deleted on <date>”, with no other data.
  • Anonymous game statistics (mining time, depth, level), with no link at all to your account, to balance the game.
  • Announcements already posted on the game's Discord server (for example a rare item found) are not removed automatically. You can ask for them to be removed at the same address.
