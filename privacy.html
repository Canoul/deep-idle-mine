Privacy policy

Deep Mine Idle collects as little data as possible: no email address, no advertising, no selling of data.

Who is responsible for your data

Geoffrey Boilay, publisher of the game Deep Mine Idle (deepmineidle.fr website and “Deep Mine Idle MMO” apps). Contact: contact@deepmineidle.fr.

We do not collect any real name, email address, phone number or location.

What other players can see

Your username, your account ID, your online presence, your global and session chat messages, your records in the leaderboards and, for your friends only, your gear. Some events (rare item obtained, tower opened) are announced with your username on the game's Discord server.

Legal basis

This processing is necessary to provide you with the game you have chosen to use (performance of a contract, article 6.1.b of the GDPR). Technical logs and moderation rely on our legitimate interest in protecting the game and its players (article 6.1.f).

Where your data is stored

  • Game server: OVHcloud, in France.
  • Database: Neon, in London (United Kingdom). This transfer outside the European Union relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the United Kingdom.
  • Announcements: Discord (United States), only the username and the name of the item or event.
  • Moderation: a private Discord channel, reserved for the team, receives each report (usernames, reason, comment and reported text).

Your data is not sold, rented or shared for advertising purposes.

Storage in your browser or on your device

The game saves your login token and your display preferences (background, first steps guide, open tab) on your device. This storage is only used to run the game: there are no advertising or audience measurement cookies, so no consent banner.

Your rights

You can at any time access your data, correct it, delete it, object to processing, ask for it to be restricted or get a copy of it. To delete your account and all its data: in the game, My account → Delete my account, or write to contact@deepmineidle.fr giving your account ID. We reply within one month.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can contact the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).

Minors

The game is open from age 13. Between 13 and 15, a parent's consent is required. A parent can ask for their child's account to be deleted at contact@deepmineidle.fr.

Changes

We may change this policy. The date of the last update is at the top of the page, and important changes are announced in the game.
